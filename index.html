What this is

We opened Car Auto Play the way a new driver does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and revenue across the market, not inside one app. We pointed that at Car Auto Play. We installed it, went through the first screens up to the dashboard, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people.

What follows is five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours: the night-blue screens, the brand logo tiles, the Brand / Model / Year pills, the dial loader, the theme cards, the plan cards and your blue Continue button. Only the order changed, plus a few things you already have but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If you have already run one of these, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
