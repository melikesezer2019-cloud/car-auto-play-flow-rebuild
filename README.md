# Growth Audit — Car Auto Play

**Live:** https://melikesezer2019-cloud.github.io/car-auto-play-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Car Auto Play already has, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in the app's own design. Only the order of the screens changed, plus one new offer on close.

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Offer the free trial when they close the paywall | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Ask what they want before the price | What to do · Theme · Loader | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Sell the screen they said they have | Paywall | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Lead with yearly and show what it saves | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.5 stars where they decide | Ready | CR +10–15% |

The prototype has English, Portuguese and German, light and dark mode, and a 30-second walkthrough. It opens straight on the prototype with `?open=proto`.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating and feature line comes from Car Auto Play's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Prices are shown in euros, as they appear in the app.

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Car Auto Play.*
